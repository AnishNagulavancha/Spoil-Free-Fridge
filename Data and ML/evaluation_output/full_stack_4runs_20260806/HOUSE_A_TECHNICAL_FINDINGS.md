# House A technical findings

## Experimental scope

The final House A dataset contains three local empty-container controls and
four chicken sessions acquired with the same PCB design, physical device, and
chamber:

- S005, S006, S007: empty controls;
- S010: excluded development pilot;
- Chicken 2: confirmation 1, with the acquisition-time role corrected from
  `pilot` to `confirmation` while retaining the original value for audit;
- Chicken 3: confirmation 2; and
- Chicken 4: confirmation 3, started from a longer-refrigerated condition.

All primary comparisons use the first eight hours after the insertion/control
prompt. Chicken recordings longer than eight hours are truncated only for the
matched AUC and fixed-time comparisons. Camera results are excluded.

## Signal processing

Warmup rows are discarded. Timestamp and sensor fields are converted to
numeric values; nonfinite values, physically invalid temperature/humidity,
nonpositive BME resistance, and gas voltages outside 0–5.5 V are rejected.
Only exactly duplicated measurement records are removed. Buffered serial
measurements sharing a rounded host timestamp are retained when their sensor
values differ.

Valid raw data are reduced to one-minute medians for feature construction and
then five-minute medians for control calibration and scoring. The first 30
minutes after the prompt form each session's food/handling baseline.

For gas channel `j`, the baseline-relative feature is

```text
D_j(t) = V_j(t) / mean_baseline(V_j) - 1
```

For BME resistance, rising VOC response is represented with an inverted
log-ratio:

```text
D_BME(t) = ln(mean_baseline(R_BME) / R_BME(t))
```

The feature stage also computes least-squares rolling slopes over 5, 15, and
30 minutes, slope-of-slope acceleration, rolling standard deviation, and Q10
biological age:

```text
Q10(t) = 2^((T(t) - 4) / 10)
biological_age = integral Q10(t) dt
```

Those additional trend and Q10 features are retained for diagnostics. They do
not currently enter the frozen primary event or displayed Change Index.

## Local control calibration

The three empty controls are pooled to estimate a smoothed expected drift curve
for each baseline-relative channel. Leave-one-control-out residuals are
generated by fitting drift on two controls and applying it to the third. Robust
MAD scales from those residuals are:

| Channel | LOO robust scale |
|---|---:|
| NH3 | 0.029649 |
| H2S | 0.065507 |
| CH4 | 0.078839 |
| BME | 0.055924 |

For sensor `j`, the control-standardized response is

```text
z_j(t) = direction_j * (D_j(t) - control_drift_j(t)) / scale_j
```

All frozen direction multipliers are `+1`. Negative responses are clipped to
zero for the displayed index; they do not cancel positive channels.

Each channel is mapped to 0–1 by

```text
G_j(t) = clip(z_j(t) / 40, 0, 1)
```

The 0–100 Deterioration-Associated Change Index is

```text
Index(t) = 100 * [0.30 G_NH3 + 0.35 G_H2S + 0.10 G_CH4 + 0.25 G_BME]
```

The weights are a frozen protein-food engineering heuristic, not coefficients
estimated from the confirmation data. A channel value reaches its display
ceiling when its corrected response reaches 40 control residual scales. This
is numerical clipping, not necessarily raw sensor saturation. Index 100 does
not mean 100% spoiled.

## Primary event definition

For each standardized channel, a one-sided CUSUM is calculated on five-minute
bins:

```text
C_j(t) = max(0, C_j(t-1) + z_j(t) - k)
k = 0.5
h = 30
```

A channel becomes active after `C_j > 30` for three consecutive bins (15
minutes). The frozen primary event is

```text
(NH3 active OR H2S active) AND BME active
```

CH4 is supporting evidence and is not required. The threshold `h=30` was
selected using empty controls only. A 2,000-session moving-block bootstrap of
the control-null residuals estimated a 1.25% false-session probability for the
complete eight-hour primary rule. None of the three observed controls produced
a primary event.

CUSUM detection time is the earliest persistent departure from expected empty
behavior. It is not the biological onset of spoilage and need not coincide with
the steepest part of the displayed index curve.

| Index at 8 h | 79.31 | 89.32 | 80.04 | 87.28 |

All four chicken sessions satisfy the primary event rule. The three local
controls have no primary events, maximum indices of 0.39–4.56, and eight-hour
AUC values of 0.47–10.92. Chicken maxima are 89.99–91.71 and chicken AUC values
are 206.48–313.48. Thus there is no observed overlap between control and
chicken sessions for maximum index or eight-hour AUC. The minimum chicken AUC
is approximately 18.9 times the maximum control AUC.

This apparent 4/4 chicken detection and 0/3 control detection must not be
reported as 100% diagnostic accuracy: the sample is very small, all data come
from one local setup, the pilot is development data, and no independent
biological labels are available.

## Repeatability statistics

Across the three confirmation sessions only:

- detection latency: mean 2.50 h, SD 1.09 h, range 1.75–3.75 h;
- index at hour 4: mean 17.35, SD 3.83;
- index at hour 6: mean 52.67, SD 24.39;
- index at hour 8: mean 85.54, SD 4.88; and
- AUC 0–8 h: mean 261.36 index-hours, SD 53.55.

Across all four chicken trajectories descriptively:

- detection latency: 2.90 ± 1.19 h;
- index at hour 4: 17.79 ± 3.25;
- index at hour 6: 59.58 ± 24.24;
- index at hour 8: 83.99 ± 5.06; and
- AUC 0–8 h: 268.89 ± 46.24 index-hours.

The large hour-6 variance and much smaller hour-8 variance show that session
timing varies substantially but the eventual eight-hour response is more
repeatable.

## Rapid-response phase versus CUSUM onset

Three trajectories begin their large rise around 3.5–4 hours and remain above
index 50 for three consecutive bins by 4.92–5.00 hours. The longer-refrigerated
confirmation remains above 50 only at 6.83 hours. This descriptive threshold
was calculated after observing the trajectories and is not a frozen biological
decision boundary.

Accordingly, the data support two different time concepts:

1. **CUSUM response onset:** earliest sustained multi-channel departure from
   empty-control behavior, 1.75–4.08 hours.
2. **Large-response phase:** strong increase in corrected response magnitude,
   near five hours for the first three trajectories and later for the
   refrigerated sample.

Neither quantity is a proven time at which the food "became spoiled."
Deterioration is continuous, and a biological transition requires independent
ground truth.

## Unsupervised diagnostics

PCA and Isolation Forest are fitted only to locally standardized control-null
data. During hours 4–8, every five-minute bin in every chicken session is
classified anomalous by Isolation Forest. Whole-window chicken anomaly
fractions are 88.7–99.0%.

The median absolute PC1 coordinate for control-null data is approximately 0.69.
During chicken hours 4–8, median absolute PC1 is 11.60–32.29. This demonstrates
large multivariate separation from the control state. PCA direction and
Isolation Forest anomaly status do not provide a probability of spoilage.

## Environmental and independence limitations

Within chicken sessions, corrected gas/BME trajectories are often highly
correlated. This can reflect shared biological headspace response, common
humidity/temperature sensitivity, or sensor cross-sensitivity. The four
channels therefore cannot be counted as four independent biological lines of
evidence.

The refrigerated confirmation illustrates this limitation: its early BME
response has first-difference correlation of approximately 0.49 with humidity
and 0.46 with temperature. Its primary CUSUM event occurs at 2.00 hours, while
the large NH3/H2S/BME rise occurs much later. Temperature/humidity compensation
or an explicit confidence penalty remains necessary for deployment.

BME temperature is PCB/chamber-adjacent, approximately 30–31 C, rather than
food core temperature. Q10 biological age derived from it is supporting
metadata and should not be treated as food-age ground truth.

## Technical conclusion

The frozen House A algorithm reproducibly distinguishes chicken sessions from
local empty-container behavior. All three independent confirmations and the
excluded pilot exhibit a sustained protein-gas-plus-BME event, large
control-adjusted AUC, and strong PCA/Isolation-Forest separation. The most
repeatable descriptive endpoint is the high eight-hour Change Index, not a
fixed four-hour biological transition.

The evidence supports the statement:

> In this PCB/chamber/household setup, chicken exposure produced a repeatable,
> sustained, control-adjusted multi-sensor headspace response substantially
> exceeding empty-container drift.

The evidence does not establish microbiological spoilage, pathogen presence,
edibility, a safety threshold, percentage spoiled, remaining useful life, or
generalization to another household. Those claims require independent ground
truth and external validation with a frozen model.
